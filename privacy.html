Kanchuan Privacy Policy

Effective November 17, 2026

In short
  • Kanchuan reads your screen only after you start screen sharing, and only until you stop it.
  • Reading and translating happen on your iPhone. Your screen is never sent to us or anyone else.
  • We don't collect personal data, use analytics or ads, or track you.

What Kanchuan does with your screen

Kanchuan translates Chinese apps for you. To do that, you start iOS screen sharing (a "broadcast") from inside Kanchuan. While it runs, iOS shows a red indicator and Kanchuan receives images of your screen.

Kanchuan reads the text in those images using Apple's on-device text recognition, translates Chinese text using Apple's on-device translation, and shows the result in a floating window. Images of your screen are processed in memory and discarded immediately. They are never saved, uploaded or shared.

The text from the most recent screen is kept briefly in Kanchuan's private storage on your device so the app and its screen-sharing extension can pass it to each other. It is replaced with every update and deleted when you stop screen sharing.

You can stop screen sharing at any time from Kanchuan, from Control Center, or by tapping the red indicator. We recommend pausing before you open banking or payment screens.

Data we collect

None. Kanchuan has no accounts, and contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code. We don't receive your screen content, translations, location, contacts or any other personal information.

Services provided by Apple

  • Translation languages: the first time you use a language, iOS may download its translation files from Apple. That download is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Purchases: trip passes are bought through the App Store. Apple processes your payment; we never see your payment details.
  • Crash reports: if you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports.

Children

Kanchuan isn't directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If a future version handles data differently (for example, an optional online feature), we'll update this policy and the date above before that version is released, and the feature will be off until you turn it on.
