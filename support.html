Getting started

  1. Before your trip, open Kanchuan once on Wi-Fi so it can download the offline Chinese translation pack.
  2. Tap the screen-sharing button, choose Kanchuan, then Start Broadcast.
  3. Open any Chinese app. The floating window appears and translates what's on screen as you scroll.

Common questions

The floating window doesn't appear

Make sure screen sharing is on (a red indicator shows at the top of the screen), then open Kanchuan and tap "Open floating window now". Picture in Picture must be allowed in Settings → General → Picture in Picture.

Translations show "…" or don't update

The translation pack may still be downloading. Open Kanchuan on Wi-Fi and wait for step 1 to show a tick. Translations update when you stop scrolling.

Can I tap the translation?

No. iOS doesn't allow any app to pass taps through to another app. Read the translation, then tap the matching spot in the Chinese app. Drag the window aside or push it to the screen edge to tuck it away.

Does it work without internet or a VPN?

Yes. Once the translation pack is downloaded, reading and translating happen entirely on your iPhone.

Is my screen private?

Yes. Nothing on your screen leaves your iPhone. See the privacy policy.
